Calendars on the start page

Your calendars

Events show on the start page calendar and refresh every 15 minutes. View only: add or change events in Google, Outlook or iCloud. The links are kept on this PC only.

Add a calendar

Paste the calendar's private iCal link (it ends in .ics, or starts with webcal://).

Where to find the link

Google Calendar
  1. On a computer, open calendar.google.com, then the gear → Settings.
  2. On the left, pick your calendar under Settings for my calendars.
  3. Under Integrate calendar, copy Secret address in iCal format.
Outlook / Microsoft 365
  1. In Outlook on the web: gear → Calendar → Shared calendars.
  2. Under Publish a calendar, choose the calendar and Can view all details, then Publish.
  3. Copy the ICS link.
iCloud (Apple)
  1. On iCloud.com or an iPhone, open Calendar and tap the share icon next to the calendar.
  2. Turn on Public Calendar.
  3. Copy the link (it starts with webcal://).
Yahoo and others
  1. Look for Share, Export or Subscribe in the calendar's settings.
  2. Copy the iCal / ICS link.
